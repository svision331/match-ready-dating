FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Have more questions?
Do I need to have outfits ready?
No. If you're unsure what to wear, the pre-shoot style consult (included in Tier 2 and 3) covers exactly that. You can also send photos of what you're thinking and I'll give feedback before shoot day.
What if I've never had professional photos taken?
Most of my clients haven't. The entire session is directed — I tell you where to stand, how to move, and when to look at the camera. The first 15 minutes are always a warm-up.
How do I pick the right package?
If you just need better photos, Starter. If your whole profile needs work, Overhaul. If you want everything handled — style, grooming, bio — Full Treatment. When in doubt, Overhaul is the right call.
What's the cancellation policy?
Cancel 72+ hours out for a full refund. 24–72 hours converts to a 6-month credit. Under 24 hours or no-show, the deposit is forfeited. One free reschedule with 72+ hours notice — $35 fee on a second.
Do you shoot outside your home city?
Yes. A travel fee of $99–$150 applies outside my home city. I batch shoots in each city over a weekend trip — fill out the form and I'll confirm availability.
Do you share before/after photos publicly?
Only with explicit opt-in permission on the intake form. Usernames and identifying bio text are always blurred. You can withdraw consent any time.
